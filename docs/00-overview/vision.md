## Vision

Build the digital operating layer for civil society organizations, with a content
platform at its centre and extensible operational modules around it.

## Mission

Enable an NGO to manage its public presence, internal information, projects,
people, partners, programs, evidence, documents, workflows, and reporting from one
coherent platform without sacrificing openness or interoperability.

## Positioning

> A Digital Operating Platform for NGOs, powered by a modular CMS and an
> API-first architecture.

**Not:** a WordPress clone · a website builder only · a donor CRM only · an ERP
replacement on day one · a data-collection app only.

## Target Organizations

Local NGOs, international NGOs, non-profits, civil-society organizations,
community-based organizations, associations and foundations, social enterprises
with non-profit programs, research and advocacy organizations, humanitarian and
development organizations, media/civil-society initiatives, networks and
coalitions, multi-branch organizations.

## Primary Product Principles

1. API-first — every important capability is available through a versioned API.
2. Modular — functionality is delivered as independent modules.
3. Multi-tenant — one platform hosts multiple organizations with strong isolation.
4. Arabic-first — Arabic RTL is a first-class experience; English and more supported.
5. Headless-capable — websites, portals, mobile apps, and external systems share the same APIs.
6. Configurable — custom fields, workflows, forms, permissions, themes, modules,
   dashboards, and content blocks without code where practical.
7. Secure by design — RBAC, ABAC, MFA, audit logging, data protection, backups,
   least privilege as foundational capabilities.
8. Human-controlled AI — AI assists; it never silently becomes an authoritative record.
9. Self-hostable — reference deployment on Linux + Docker Compose, with a path to scale.
10. Open integration model — REST, webhooks, OAuth/OIDC, and provider adapters.
